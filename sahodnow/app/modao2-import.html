How interest works

  • Equal principal & equal interest — same principal each installment; interest based on the original loan amount.
  • First installment: interest accrues daily (10–40 days) on the full principal.
  • Later installments: fixed interest per installment on the full principal.
  • If fewer than 10 days remain to the next due date, the first payment moves to the following billing period.

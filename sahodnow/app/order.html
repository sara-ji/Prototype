Repayment terms

How we calculate your loan, early payoff, and late fees.

Interest calculation

  • Equal principal & equal interest — the same principal each installment; interest is based on your original loan amount.
  • First installment: daily interest for the actual days until your first due date (10–40 days) on the full principal.
  • Later installments: fixed monthly interest on the full principal, plus the service fee shown in your plan.
  • If fewer than 10 days remain to the next due date, your first payment moves to the following billing period.

Early repayment

If you pay off your loan early, the full principal, interest, and fees for all remaining installments are still charged. There is no discount for early payoff at this time.

Late payment penalties

  • Penalty interest: 0.16% per day, up to 5% per month.
  • Total penalties are capped at 1× your loan principal.

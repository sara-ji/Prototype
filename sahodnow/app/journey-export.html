• Privacy & Permissions

Before we begin

KitaMo needs your consent to collect device information, location (approximate), and usage data for credit assessment, fraud prevention, and attribution per Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).

  • Device ID & app install source
  • Network & carrier information
  • Camera (for KYC verification only)
  • SMS (OTP verification only)

• Your privacy matters

We only ask to help you borrow

Permissions let us verify it's you and set a fair credit limit. That's the only reason we collect them.

  • 🛡️
    For your limit only Device & usage data go straight into your credit assessment — nothing else.
  • 🔒
    Never sold. Never shared. Your info stays inside KitaMo. We don't pass it to advertisers or other companies.
  • ✓
    You're in control Camera & SMS are only used when you verify ID or log in — you tap to allow each time.

This Agreement is entered into between KitaMo Lending Inc. (SEC Registration No. CS2019XXXX) and the Borrower identified in the KYC records.

1. Credit Line. Borrower is granted a revolving credit facility up to the approved limit. Each draw creates a separate loan subject to the terms selected at disbursement.

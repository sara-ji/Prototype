Repayment guide

• Fixed repayment day

SahodNow uses a fixed repayment schedule. Each month, your statement is generated and payment is due on the 20th. If you have multiple loans, several installments may be due on the same date.

How to repay

1 Open Bills → Repay
2 Choose a channel (GCash / Maya / Bank)
3 Complete payment and confirm in the app

In this prototype, payment confirmation is simulated and does not change balances automatically.

Safety checklist

OTP / PIN Never share
Payment link Only from inside the app
Personal account Never transfer to individuals

About us

A partner you can trust.

Lendwise Financing Corp. is an SEC-registered financing company. Through SahodNow, we provide digital installment credit for salaried professionals in the Philippines.

Who we are

Lendwise Financing Corp.

Lendwise Financing Corp. is a corporation organized under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines. We are a financing company registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

SahodNow is our digital lending platform. It is how we extend installment credit to employed Filipinos — people with stable income and a known payday — so household cash flow can keep pace with life between pay dates.

Our focus is responsible consumer finance: clear pricing, underwriting grounded in salary, and repayment structured around the day our customers get paid.

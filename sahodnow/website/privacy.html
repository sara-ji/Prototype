Privacy Policy

How SahodNow Lending Inc. collects and uses personal data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Last updated: August 2026 · Summary for website visitors. Full legal text may be published as PDF.

Who we are

SahodNow Lending Inc. (“SahodNow,” “we,” “us”) provides revolving credit services through the SahodNow mobile application in the Philippines.

What we collect

  • Identity and contact information you provide during registration and KYC
  • Government ID images and biometric data for verification
  • Income and employment-related documents if you choose credit enhancement
  • Device, app usage, and fraud-prevention signals as disclosed at first launch
  • Loan, repayment, and transaction records related to your account

How we use your data

We use your information to verify identity, assess creditworthiness, process loans and repayments, prevent fraud, comply with law, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing.

Sharing

We may share data with regulated service providers (payment, messaging, cloud hosting, credit bureaus where permitted) only as needed to operate the service and under appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data subject to legal and contractual requirements. Contact privacy@sahodnow.ph or use in-app customer service.

Security

We employ encryption and access controls appropriate to financial services. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; please protect your account credentials.

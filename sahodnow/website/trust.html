Data privacy

How we keep your data safe.

Your information is used for lending and fraud prevention. It is not sold. It is not collected in a chat. Below is what that means in practice.

01

We ask before we collect

The app explains each permission — what we need and why — before the system prompt. Camera is required to verify your identity with your ID and a face scan. Other permissions can be declined and you can still continue.

02

We only take what lending needs

Data is collected to assess your application, send funds, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing lists.

03

Your data is encrypted

Information is encrypted as it moves between your phone and our systems. Access is limited to people and partners who need it to serve your loan.

04

We follow the Data Privacy Act

Lendwise operates under Republic Act No. 10173 and National Privacy Commission rules: collect for a stated purpose, keep it no longer than needed, and respect your rights to access, correction, and complaint.

05

You can ask about your data

For access, correction, or a privacy complaint, email privacy@sahodnow.ph. We will never ask for your OTP, PIN, or a personal wallet in that process.

Stay safe

How you can protect yourself.

Official app only
Download SahodNow from Google Play or the App Store. We do not take applications on Viber, Facebook comments, or through a “verifier” or “agent” in chat.
Never share OTP or PIN
A real SahodNow representative will never ask for your OTP, PIN, or password by SMS, call, or messaging app. If someone does, it is a scam.
Applying is free
No one is allowed to charge an assist fee, processing fee, or “speed-up” payment outside the app. If someone asks you to send money to approve a loan, it is a scam.
Use official channels
support@sahodnow.ph for your account. privacy@sahodnow.ph for data rights. If a number or page is not listed on this website, it is not us.

Lendwise Financing Corp. is an SEC-registered financing company. SahodNow is our digital lending platform for salaried professionals in the Philippines.

Who we are

Lendwise Financing Corp.

A corporation organized under Philippine law. We extend installment credit to employed Filipinos with stable income and a known payday.

We underwrite on salary, disclose pricing before confirmation, and structure repayment around the day our customers get paid.

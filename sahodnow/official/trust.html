Data privacy

How we keep your data safe.

Used for lending and fraud prevention. Never sold.

01

We ask before we collect

The app explains each permission — what we need and why — before the system prompt. Camera is required for ID and a face scan. Other permissions can be declined.

02

We only take what lending needs

To assess your application, send funds, and prevent fraud — not for unrelated marketing.

03

Your data is encrypted

Encrypted in transit. Access is limited to people and partners who service your loan.

04

We follow the Data Privacy Act

RA 10173 and NPC rules: collect for a stated purpose, keep it no longer than needed, and honor your rights to access, correction, and complaint.

05

You can ask about your data

Email privacy@sahodnow.ph. We will never ask for your OTP, PIN, or a personal wallet.

Stay safe

How you can protect yourself.

Official app only
Download SahodNow from Google Play or the App Store. We do not take applications on Viber, Facebook, or through an “agent” in chat.
Never share OTP or PIN
A SahodNow representative will never ask for your OTP, PIN, or password by SMS, call, or chat. If someone does, it is a scam.
Applying is free
No assist fee, processing fee, or “speed-up” payment outside the app. If someone asks you to send money to approve a loan, it is a scam.
Use official channels
support@sahodnow.ph for your account. privacy@sahodnow.ph for data rights. If it is not on this website, it is not us.

Data sharing

Shared only when the loan requires it.

GCash, Maya, banks
To send funds, on the channel you choose in the app.
Credit Information Corporation
To read credit standing, as required in the Philippines.
SEC and NPC
Our regulators for lending and data privacy.
Google Play, App Store
The only stores where the official SahodNow app is listed.

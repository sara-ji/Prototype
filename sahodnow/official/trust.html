Your data

How we protect your data.

01

We ask first

We collect nothing until you allow it. The camera is required, for your ID and a face scan. Decline anything else and you can still apply.

02

Only what lending needs

To review the application, send funds, and stop fraud. Not for unrelated marketing.

03

Encrypted, limited access

Protected in transit. Only people and partners who service the loan can see it.

04

Data Privacy Act

RA 10173 and NPC rules. Collected for a stated purpose, kept only as long as needed. You can ask to access or correct it.

Stay safe

If it is not in the app, it is not us.

Official app only

Google Play or the App Store. No applications on Viber, Facebook, or through an agent in chat.

Never share an OTP or PIN

We will not ask for your OTP, PIN, or password by SMS, call, or chat.

Applying is free

No assist fee, processing fee, or payment to “speed up” approval. Sending money to get a loan is a scam.
